Contract Renewal & Cancellation Guard

Every contract gets one headline date: the last day to cancel. It is the end date minus the notice period, moved earlier when it lands on a weekend or public holiday. Reminders, alerts and the dashboard all count down to it.

Free add-on: Contract Guard - Vendor Bill Matching checks your vendor bills against each contract. It is listed separately on the Odoo Apps store, costs nothing and needs Accounting.

Features

Know before it renews
  • Alerts with the money in them: "Office lease renews in 14 days for $4,200. Cancel by Friday."
  • Multi-stage reminders (default 90 / 60 / 30 / 7 days) by Odoo activity and email, plus a Monday digest.
  • Uses your company working calendar and public holidays.
  • Optional Slack, Google Chat, Discord and Teams alerts through simple webhooks.
Act before the deadline
  • Notice letter in one click: edit the text, create a PDF on your letterhead, email it and file it on the contract.
  • Renewal decisions: renew, renegotiate or cancel, with a reason and manager approval.
  • An agreed new price carries into the renewed contract.
  • Amendments with history and a renewal chain.
Prove the savings
  • Savings counter: adds up what you saved by cancelling or renegotiating.
  • Price escalation (fixed % or scheduled step-ups) with the projected cost of the whole contract.
  • 12-month commitment forecast as pivot and graph.
  • Bill matching with the free add-on Contract Guard - Vendor Bill Matching (needs Accounting): flags overcharges, silent price increases and missing bills.

Also included: vendor, customer, lease, warranty, license, NDA and service contracts in one place, confidential contracts, access groups, multi-company, multi-currency, and CSV import with a ready-made template.

FAQ

Does it work on Community and Enterprise?
Yes, both. It needs only Discuss (mail) and Analytic.

Do I need Accounting?
No. Accounting is only needed for bill matching, which comes from the free add-on Contract Guard - Vendor Bill Matching (listed separately on the Odoo Apps store). Once the add-on is in your addons folder and Accounting is installed, it turns on automatically. It only reads vendor bills and never creates or changes an accounting entry.

How is the last day to cancel calculated?
End date minus the notice period, moved earlier if it falls on a weekend or public holiday in your company calendar.

Do webhooks need setup?
They are off until you add a webhook URL. Confidential contracts are never included in messages, and a failing webhook never blocks a reminder or a user.

Can I import my existing contracts?
Yes. Use the CSV template that comes with the module.
