Contract Guard - Vendor Bill Matching

Your contract says one price. Is your vendor billing you another? This add-on compares the posted vendor bills of each counterparty with the payments your contract expects, and flags what does not match.

This is a free add-on. It works only together with Contract Renewal & Cancellation Guard, which you need to install first. It also needs Accounting.

What it flags

Bills above the agreed price

Overcharges and price increases nobody announced, with the difference shown.

Bills below it

Payments billed for less than agreed, so you can check that nothing was left out.

Periods with no bill

A projected payment with no vendor bill found for it stands out instead of going unnoticed.

Open any projected payment to see the agreed amount, what was actually billed, the difference, and the matched vendor bill with its payment status.

FAQ

Is it really free?
Yes. There is nothing to pay for this add-on.

Do I need the main app?
Yes. Install Contract Renewal & Cancellation Guard first. This add-on has nothing to check without its contracts and projected payments.

Does it need setup?
No. When Contract Renewal & Cancellation Guard and Accounting are both installed, the add-on installs itself and the bill check appears on your projected payments.

Does it change my accounting?
Never. It only reads posted vendor bills. It does not create, edit or post any accounting entry.

Does it work on Community and Enterprise?
Yes, both.
