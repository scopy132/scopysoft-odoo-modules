Summary

Overview

Smart Reconcile pairs customer payments with invoices and vendor payments with bills by amount and reference, tells you why each pair matched, and labels every doubtful row for your review. It groups split payments, explains short payments with your withholding tax and bank charge rules, and every reconciliation can be undone from the log.

Capabilities

Features

Exact Matches, Pre-Ticked

Amount and invoice reference both match: the row comes ticked and ready. Nothing is booked until you click Reconcile Confirmed.

Every Row Says Why

A plain-English reason sits next to each suggestion, so you can trust a match or catch a wrong one in seconds.

Confidence Badges

Exact, amount only, reference only, multiple candidates, nothing close. Doubtful rows are never ticked for you.

Split Payments

Several payments quoting one invoice are grouped. An exact total is ticked; partial or over-paid totals are left for your review.

Payments Without Reference

Suggests the 2 or 3 payments of a partner that add up exactly to an invoice. Always unticked, always low confidence.

WHT, Bank Charges, Write-offs

Rules for 5% or 10% withholding tax, flat bank fees and small rounding tolerances. The difference is booked to the account you choose.

One-Click Undo

Every reconciliation, adjustments included, is logged with who did it and why, and can be reversed from the Reconciliation Log.

Customers and Vendors

Customer payments against invoices and vendor payments against bills, from the same screen.

Dashboard and Nightly Run

Pivot and graph views of everything reconciled. An optional nightly run (off by default) books only the safe matches and emails a summary.

Tips

Good to Know

Two Ways to Launch It

You do not have to open the Auto-Match Payments menu. Select payments or invoices in any list, click Actions, then Auto-Match Payments, and only your selection is scanned.

The Nightly Summary Email Needs Outgoing Mail

The nightly run is off by default. Once on, it reconciles safe matches even without mail, but the summary email is only sent if an outgoing mail server is configured in Odoo and recipients are chosen under Send Summary To. The latest result is always kept in Settings.

Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Does anything get reconciled without my approval?

No. Matches are only booked when you click Reconcile Confirmed. The optional nightly run is off by default, and when on it only books matches where both amount and invoice reference agree (amount-only matches are a separate opt-in).

Does it work for vendor bills as well as customer invoices?

Yes. Customer payments are matched to invoices and vendor payments to bills.

What if a payment arrives short because of withholding tax or a bank fee?

Create an adjustment rule (percentage or fixed amount) with its account and journal. When the shortfall equals exactly what a rule calculates, the row is labelled Matched with Adjustment and the difference is booked for you.

Can I undo a reconciliation?

Yes. Every reconciliation is in the Reconciliation Log and can be undone in one click, adjustment entries included.

Is it multi-company?

Settings and adjustment rules are kept per company.

Walkthrough

Screenshots

01. Open Smart Reconcile > Auto-Match Payments, leave the filters blank and click Find Matches.

02. Or select payments or invoices in any list, open Actions and click Auto-Match Payments.

03. Read the confidence badge and the Why column. Green rows are exact matches and come ticked.

04. Split payments, shortfalls explained by a rule and payment combinations are labelled so you know what to double check.

05. Vendor bills work the same way: launch it from bills and payments.

06. Untick anything you are unsure about and click Reconcile Confirmed.

07. Set up Adjustment Rules once: withholding tax, bank charges and small write-offs.

08. Pick the type, rate or fixed amount, account and journal, and optionally limit a rule to certain partners.

09. Every reconciliation lands in the Reconciliation Log, with its match type, the reason and an Undo link on each row.

10. Open any log entry to see who did it, why it matched, the breakdown and the adjustment entry. Click Undo Reconciliation to reverse it.

11. The Dashboard shows what was reconciled over time, as a chart, pivot or list.

12. Under Settings, turn on the optional Nightly Auto-Run, choose who gets the summary email, or click Run Nightly Job Now.
