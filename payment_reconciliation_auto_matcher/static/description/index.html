Payment Reconciliation Auto-Matcher

Stop matching payments to invoices one at a time. Scan, review, and reconcile a whole batch in the time it used to take you to do one.

Every payment that comes in has to be matched to the right invoice or bill before your books are actually correct. Doing that by hand, one payment at a time, checking the amount, checking the reference, scrolling through open invoices, is slow and it is where mistakes creep in. This module scans your unreconciled payments, scores every possible match, and hands you a review list instead of a blank search box.

How It Works

1. Start from a selection, or start from scratch

Already looking at a few payments or invoices? Select them, open Actions, and choose Auto-Match Payments. The wizard scans only what you picked.

2. Or leave every filter blank to scan everything

Prefer to just see where things stand? Go to Accounting, open Auto-Match Payments on its own (not from a selection), and click Find Matches without touching the Journal, Partner, or Date fields. It will pull in every unreconciled payment across the company and every open invoice or bill it could pair them with, no setup required.

3. Every suggestion comes with a confidence score

Amount and reference both match: it is pre-ticked and ready. Only one of the two lines up, or more than one invoice looks equally likely: it is flagged for you to pick yourself instead of being guessed at. Nothing gets reconciled without your say-so.

4. Edge cases are labeled, not hidden

Two payments that could both fit one invoice, a payment with nothing close to it, an invoice already claimed elsewhere in the same batch: each one gets its own clear badge so you know exactly which rows need a second look before you confirm anything.

5. Customer payments and vendor bills, both covered

It works in both directions: customer payments against open invoices, and vendor payments against open bills, even when the payment reference and the bill number do not match up.

6. Confirm once, reconcile the whole batch

Untick anything you are not sure about, then hit Reconcile Confirmed. Everything else gets reconciled together in one click instead of one payment at a time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it handle both customer payments and vendor bills?

Yes. It scans customer payments against open invoices and vendor payments against open bills, in either direction depending on where you launch it from.

How does the matching actually work?

Each candidate pair is scored on two signals: whether the amount matches the open balance, and whether the invoice number or reference appears in the payment memo. Both signals matching gives the highest confidence.

What happens when it is not sure?

Nothing is ticked automatically. The row is flagged (multiple possible matches, nothing close, or already claimed by a stronger match in the same batch) and left for you to confirm or repick.

Will it ever reconcile the wrong thing automatically?

No. Every row, confident or not, has to be ticked and confirmed before anything is reconciled. You are always the last check.

What if I do not set any filters at all?

Launch it on its own from Accounting instead of from a selection, leave Journal, Partner, and the date range blank, and click Find Matches. It scans every unreconciled payment and every open invoice or bill company-wide.

Does it support multiple currencies?

Matching is done within a single currency at a time. Payments are only compared against invoices and bills in the same currency.
