Stock Restrictions & Controls

Block the mistakes before they hit your warehouse.

Every warehouse has a story like this

A receiving clerk fat-fingers a quantity and stock goes negative. A junior staffer deletes a draft transfer that someone else was still working on. A receipt gets created with no Purchase Order behind it, and nobody notices until the numbers stop matching.

None of this is malicious. It is just what happens when Odoo lets anyone do anything to a stock operation, at any time. This module puts a guardrail on each of those moments, with one toggle each.

One settings page. Every guardrail.

Every restriction lives in Inventory to Configuration to Settings. Flip what you need, leave the rest off. No code, no extra menus to learn.

10 Guardrails, One Toggle Each

1. Block manual receipts without a Purchase Order

Stops the mistake: receipts appearing out of nowhere, with no paper trail back to a vendor order.

2. Block manual deliveries without a Sales Order

Stops the mistake: stock walking out the door with nothing sold to explain it.

3. Block over-validation of quantities

Stops the mistake: validating 3 units when only 1 was ever demanded. Configurable tolerance percentage if you want some flexibility.

4. Block under-validation of quantities

Stops the mistake: closing out a transfer short, with nobody following up on the missing units. Shares the same tolerance setting as over-validation.

5. Block negative stock on deliveries

Stops the mistake: promising and delivering stock you do not actually have on hand.

6. Block backdating stock operations

Stops the mistake: scheduled dates quietly set in the past to make reports look cleaner than they were.

7. Only Inventory Managers can validate operations

Stops the mistake: junior staff closing out transfers that actually need a second set of eyes first.

8. Only Inventory Managers can delete draft operations

Stops the mistake: a draft transfer someone else was still working on disappearing without a trace.

9. Block receiving from unregistered vendors

Stops the mistake: stock coming in from a vendor nobody approved for that product, bypassing your purchasing process entirely.

10. Complete restriction audit log

Every blocked attempt is recorded automatically: who tried it, what they tried, and when. Filterable by restriction type so a manager can review a history of exactly what has been getting blocked.

Nothing gets silently overridden

Administrators always bypass every restriction. That is intentional; someone needs an escape hatch when a real exception comes up. The difference is that every single bypass is written to the same audit log as every blocked attempt, tagged with an Admin Bypass flag and filterable by restriction type. Nothing happens quietly. If a rule got skipped, there is a record of exactly who skipped it and why it mattered enough to check.

FAQ

Do I have to turn on every restriction?

No. Each one is an independent toggle. Turn on only what fits your workflow.

Can Administrators still get past a restriction if there is a real exception?

Yes, always. Every bypass is written to the audit log so it stays visible instead of silent.

Why does the Odoo 19 version have 10 restrictions instead of 12?

Odoo 19 changed how cancellation works: a validated transfer no longer uses a Cancel button, it uses Return instead, which made a dedicated cancel-restriction unnecessary on this version. Odoo 19 also added its own native check against validating a stock operation with zero quantity, which covers that case out of the box. Odoo 16, 17, and 18 do not have either of these native behaviors, so those versions ship the full 12 restrictions.

Does this work with multi-company setups?

Yes. Every restriction is configured per company.

Will this slow down my inventory team?

Only when they are about to do something the rules say should not happen. Everything else works exactly as it did before.

What happens to the audit log entries over time?

They are kept indefinitely unless you archive or delete them yourself. Nothing is auto-purged.
